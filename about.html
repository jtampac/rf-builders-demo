About The Company

Engineering & Construction, Done Right

RF Builders & Supply is a general engineering and construction contractor based in Santa Catalina, Negros Oriental. What began as a construction materials supplier has grown into an integrated operation that designs and delivers infrastructure while supplying the aggregates and materials that build it.

We undertake building, road, and bridge construction, site development, and specialty works such as pickleball court construction. Backed by our own quarry, heavy equipment fleet, and experienced crews, we serve government agencies, developers, resort owners, commercial clients, contractors, and private project owners with professional, accountable execution.
